Everything in Tonality, Rhythm and Breath is read when a phrase begins and held until it ends, so a change is heard from the next phrase.



  

    
Tide breathes in phrases. A short motif is sung by the melody, then varied:
      inverted, played backwards, slowed, or broken into a fragment that climbs, and each phrase
      changes it a little so the line grows out of itself. The music decides when to rest: a phrase
      ends when it arrives on the root or fifth of its chord. It prepares the rest (the textures
      thin and ring longer, the last note is held), and a longer or louder phrase earns a longer
      silence. During a rest nothing new begins. A counter-melody moves against the melody from behind you, and the bass
      and chords follow one progression.

    
The pitched voices are physical models: the melody is a flute played with breath, the
      counter a bowed string, the bass a long plucked string, and the chords an aeolian harp
      (wind across strings). Drops are a water
      bubble striking a cup, shimmer is shards touching, the waves are filtered
      noise, and a flyer passes close with Doppler.

    
With binaural on, each voice is placed around your head with measured head-related
      transfer functions (hrtfmove2) inside a binaural room
      (hrtfearly reflections and an hrtfreverb tail).

    
Binaural test plays one isolated source four ways, about 50 seconds:
      A plain stereo pan, B dry HRTF, C HRTF in a binaural room, D the Doppler flyer.
