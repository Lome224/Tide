TIDE

a generative soundscape, synthesised live in your browser with Csound

Starts quietly and fades in. Headphones recommended.

Drag across the bars like a Max multislider. A voice's button is underlined while it is playing a phrase. Level is its volume in decibels (0 is as designed, the top is +12, the bottom is off) and is heard at once. Activity is how many of the notes in its rhythm row it plays; a changed bar turns yellow until that voice begins its next phrase, which is when the change is heard.

Changing the root or the harmony asks every voice to stop after its current phrase. When they are all silent (about 20 seconds), the piece begins again in the new key.

Each bar is how long this voice rests after one of its phrases: bar 1 after its 1st, and so on; after the 8th it starts again. Taller = longer (about 1 to 9 seconds). A full bar reads WAIT: the voice stays silent until counter, bass and chords are all waiting. Then the harmony changes and everyone begins again together.


  


  

    
Tide is seven voices, each with its own cycle: a phrase, a rest, and again. The cycles
      are slightly different lengths, so the voices start together, drift apart, and now and then
      gather in a shared silence before starting again in a new key.

    
There are two kinds of harmony. One follows Debussy: everything is whole-tone or pentatonic,
      changing only in a shared silence. The other is dark: polychords (a major triad set over a
      root and fifth a tritone or a sixth away) moved as a constant structure, down a minor third
      each time, inside the half-whole scale. In both, home moves from note to note, the bass
      holds a pedal, and phrases end unresolved.

    
The pitched voices are physical models: a bowed string, a plucked string and an aeolian harp.
      Drops are water landing on glazed porcelain, shimmer is shards touching, the
      waves are filtered noise, and a flyer passes close with Doppler. With binaural
      on, each voice is placed around your head inside a binaural room.
